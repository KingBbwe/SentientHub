# The Chronicles of Auld Ewe - Character Creation Guide: Temperament System

## Core System Parameters

### Overview
In the lands of Auld Ewe, six primordial currents flow through all beings, shaping their essence and personality. These currents combine to form twelve distinct soul-patterns that define a character's core temperament.

### The Six Currents
1. The Seeker's Stream (Investigator)
2. The Hermit's Hollow (Hermit)
3. The Catalyst's Flame (Catalyst)
4. The Weaver's Web (Networker)
5. The Rebel's Storm (Heretic)
6. The Beacon's Light (Exemplar)

### Character Creation Process

#### Step 1: Determining Your Profile
1. Roll 2d6 (two six-sided dice)
2. Create a fraction using the results: (left die)/(right die)
3. If the combination isn't valid, keep the left number and reroll the right die until you get a valid combination

#### Step 2: Valid Profile Combinations
1. Investigating Catalyst (1/3)
2. Investigating Networker (1/4)
3. Hermit Networker (2/4)
4. Hermit Heretic (2/5)
5. Catalyst Heretic (3/5)
6. Catalyst Exemplar (3/6)
7. Networker Exemplar (4/6)
8. Networker Investigator (4/1)
9. Heretic Investigator (5/1)
10. Heretic Hermit (5/2)
11. Exemplar Hermit (6/2)
12. Exemplar Catalyst (6/3)

### Soul-Pattern Manifestations

#### For Draftee (Unclassed) Characters
The profile manifests as one of the following twelve patterns:
1. Seeker of Trials (1/3)
2. Lore Weaver (1/4)
3. Quiet Bridge (2/4)
4. Whispering Rebel (2/5)
5. Trial-Forged Rebel (3/5)
6. Wandering Beacon (3/6)
7. Gathering Light (4/6)
8. Questioning Thread (4/1)
9. Storm-Bringer Seeker (5/1)
10. Secluded Storm (5/2)
11. Hermit's Lantern (6/2)
12. Crucible of Wisdom (6/3)

#### For Classed Characters
Upon ascending to Classed status, the profiles become formal titles:
1. Seeker of Trials (1/3)
2. Knowledge Weaver (1/4)
3. Secluded Nexus (2/4)
4. Secluded Iconoclast (2/5)
5. Trailblazing Iconoclast (3/5)
6. Crucible of Enlightenment (3/6)
7. Nexus of Enlightenment (4/6)
8. Lore Network Weaver (4/1)
9. Paradigm-Shifting Scholar (5/1)
10. Secluded Revolutionary (5/2)
11. Secluded Sage (6/2)
12. Crucible of Sage-hood (6/3)

### Core Profile Characteristics

Each profile combination manifests specific traits and tendencies that should be reflected in character behavior and development:

1. Investigating Catalyst (1/3)
- Driven by curiosity and experiential learning
- Learns through trial and error
- Combines scholarly pursuit with resilience

2. Investigating Networker (1/4)
- Analytical and socially adept
- Excels at knowledge sharing
- Builds relationships around learning

3. Hermit Networker (2/4)
- Naturally gifted but initially reserved
- Selective social engagement
- Emerges when truly needed

4. Hermit Heretic (2/5)
- Possess unconventional wisdom
- Challenges norms from solitude
- Reluctant but influential leader

5. Catalyst Heretic (3/5)
- Learns through direct experience
- Questions established methods
- Combines adaptability with revolution

6. Catalyst Exemplar (3/6)
- Gains wisdom through trials
- Observant and introspective
- Inspires through personal growth

7. Networker Exemplar (4/6)
- Natural community builder
- Balances social and solitary needs
- Shares wisdom through connections

8. Networker Investigator (4/1)
- Combines social skills with research
- Excels at information sharing
- Uses relationships for learning

9. Heretic Investigator (5/1)
- Challenges established knowledge
- Inspires questioning in others
- Combines rebellion with research

10. Heretic Hermit (5/2)
- Revolutionary ideas developed in solitude
- Selective sharing of insights
- Powerful when engaged

11. Exemplar Hermit (6/2)
- Deep introspection and wisdom
- Prefers observation to engagement
- Profound but reserved insight

12. Exemplar Catalyst (6/3)
- Wisdom through personal trials
- Combines vision with experience
- Inspires through personal journey

### Implementation Rules

1. The profile must be determined before any other character creation steps
2. The rolled combination cannot be changed or rerolled once established
3. All character actions and development should align with their profile's core characteristics
4. Profile manifestations differ between Draftee and Classed characters as specified above
5. Profiles influence but do not absolutely restrict character behavior
6. Character growth should occur within the framework of their established profile

### System Boundaries

1. Profiles cannot be mixed or combined beyond their established pairings
2. New profiles cannot be created outside the twelve defined combinations
3. Profile characteristics must be interpreted within the established framework
4. Character development must respect profile limitations while allowing for growth
5. Profile changes are not permitted except through specific class advancement

## Profile Interpretation Guidelines

For accurate implementation of this system, all character actions, development, and narrative elements should:
1. Reference and respect the established profile definitions
2. Maintain consistency with core characteristics
3. Allow for growth within profile parameters
4. Reflect the appropriate manifestation level (Draftee vs. Classed)
5. Honor the fundamental nature of the six currents
