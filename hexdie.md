## **Instructional Tag for AI Reference: Harmonized Hex and Die System**

**IMPORTANT MECHANICS REFERENCE: TRIUMPH, CONFLICT, AND DISASTER**

When generating content, explanations, or mechanics related to the **Harmonized Hex and Die System**, ALWAYS apply the following principles:

1. **Outcome Determination:**  
   - **Triumph**, **Conflict**, or **Disaster** outcomes are determined **solely by the Die Roll**.  
   - The **Difficulty Class (DC)** does **not** determine success or failure.  

2. **DC Calculation:**  
   - The **DC** is calculated as:  
     **Hexagram Number + Die Roll + Relevant Modifier(s)**  
   - Meeting or exceeding the DC influences the **impact or severity** of the outcome but does **not** change the outcome category.

3. **Role of the Hexagram:**  
   - The drawn **i-Ching Hexagram** serves as a **narrative container** and **thematic guide**.  
   - It frames the situation and shapes how the result unfolds in the story, but does not define success or failure.

4. **Outcome Categories Defined by Die Roll:**  
   - **Triumph (High Roll):** Major success, enhanced if the DC is met/exceeded.  
   - **Conflict (Mid Roll):** Mixed results, with consequences influenced by the DC.  
   - **Disaster (Low Roll):** Significant failure, mitigated if the DC is exceeded.

5. **Narrative Impact of DC:**  
   - Exceeding the DC **amplifies** positive outcomes or **softens** negative ones.  
   - Failing to meet the DC **limits rewards** or **deepens complications** but does not alter the core outcome type.

**MANDATORY:** All explanations, mechanics, and examples involving this system must clearly reflect this distinction between **mechanical outcomes** (Die Roll) and **narrative depth** (DC and Hexagram influence).
